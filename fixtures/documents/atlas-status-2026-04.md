## Summary
Overall status is red. The Project Atlas go-live is delayed and the 1 June 2026 date is no longer achievable.

## Blockers
Blocker 1: the Northwind Payments API certification slipped by six weeks because the vendor changed its sandbox authentication, and this delay blocks Atlas end-to-end payment testing.
Blocker 2: the Atlas data migration rehearsal found 18% of legacy customer records with invalid customer IDs, and the cleanup delay pushes the Atlas migration window out by at least four weeks.
Blocker 3: the Atlas lead integration engineer was reassigned to an urgent compliance project for three weeks, which delayed the Atlas integration work further.

## Next steps
The steering committee will decide a revised go-live date at its May meeting.
