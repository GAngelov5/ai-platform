## Decisions
The committee approved a new Project Atlas go-live date of 15 August 2026, replacing 1 June 2026.
The committee recorded that the Atlas delay was caused mainly by the Northwind certification slip and the customer data quality problems found in the migration rehearsal.

## Actions
The data team will deliver a cleansed customer ID mapping by 20 June 2026.
The security review of the Atlas portal remains pending until the external penetration test is scheduled; this is a risk, not yet a confirmed delay.
