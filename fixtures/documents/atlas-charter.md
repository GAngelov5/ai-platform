## Purpose
Atlas replaces the legacy order-management system with a new customer portal and a payments integration with the vendor Northwind Payments.

## Baseline schedule
The approved baseline plans the Atlas go-live for 1 June 2026. The charter assumes the Northwind payments certification completes by 15 April 2026 and that customer data migration finishes in May.

## Sponsors
The executive sponsor is the Chief Operations Officer. The delivery lead is the Atlas programme manager.
